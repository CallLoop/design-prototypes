Messaging Service selector · buy a number via the Twilio integration

  • New required field Messaging Service on Choose number → Local / Toll-Free when the provider is Twilio. It sits on this step because the first free number is confirmed here and skips Payment.
  • Options come from getCustomerTwilioMessagingServices (name + SID). The value goes to buyPhoneNumber / reservePhoneNumber as messagingServiceSid.
  • Nothing is pre-selected, even with one service: the user always picks the service. Continue / Confirm stays disabled until a number and a service are chosen.
  • The In your account tab has no selector: those numbers keep the service they already have in Twilio, shown in the list.
  • Payment shows the chosen service with a Change link back to the previous step.
  • Use the dark bar to switch between several services, one, none and a load error, and between a paid and a first free number.
